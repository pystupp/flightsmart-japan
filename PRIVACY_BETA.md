# FlightSmart Japan — Beta Privacy Draft

**Status:** Draft for beta testing. Update this before a broad public launch to match the services actually used.

FlightSmart Japan processes travel-search inputs such as origin, destination, travel dates, passenger ages, cabin preference, and traveler priority so that flight options can be searched and compared.

The current beta is not designed to collect passport numbers, payment-card numbers, or other booking-payment credentials inside FlightSmart.

If live search is enabled, search inputs may be sent to the flight-search provider needed to return itinerary and fare information. If a third-party feedback form is enabled, information submitted through that form is handled under that provider's policies.

Hosting platforms may collect normal technical logs such as IP-related network information, browser/device information, timestamps, and application errors according to their own policies.

Do not submit sensitive personal information through the beta feedback form. Before public launch, identify the exact hosting, analytics, API, and feedback providers used and publish a final privacy policy that accurately describes data handling, retention, and contact information.
