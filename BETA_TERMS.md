# FlightSmart Japan — Beta Use & Limitations Draft

FlightSmart Japan is an experimental decision-support tool. Its scores and explanations are informational comparisons, not guarantees, official airline ratings, or predictions that a specific future flight will or will not be delayed.

Historical operating evidence may cover different time periods and may be unavailable for some carriers or routes. When evidence is unavailable, FlightSmart may reweight available components rather than assign an artificial zero.

Live fares and availability can change or expire. Always verify the final itinerary, operating carrier, fare, baggage allowance, change/refund rules, travel documents, entry requirements, and other booking conditions with the booking provider and relevant official sources before purchasing travel.

FlightSmart does not currently act as the airline and should not be treated as providing operational, immigration, legal, insurance, or safety advice.

This document is a beta draft, not legal advice. Review and replace it with appropriate final terms before commercial launch.
