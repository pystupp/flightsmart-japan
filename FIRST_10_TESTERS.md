# First 10 Testers Plan

The first beta is for learning, not growth. Ask 10 real U.S.–Japan travelers to complete one realistic flight-search task and tell you where the app helped or confused them.

## Suggested mix

Aim for a mix such as:

- 3 Japanese-speaking adults traveling alone or as a couple
- 3 parents traveling with children
- 2 travelers who normally prioritize price
- 1 traveler who strongly prefers nonstop/fewer connections
- 1 English-first traveler who travels between the U.S. and Japan

## Tester task

Ask each tester to:

1. Open FlightSmart on their own phone or computer.
2. Choose Japanese or English.
3. Enter a real trip they might take.
4. Select the traveler priority that best matches them.
5. Review the top recommendation and one alternative.
6. Open “Why this flight?” / 「この便をおすすめする理由」.
7. Tell you whether they would trust the recommendation enough to continue to a booking site.

## Feedback questions

Use these questions in Google Forms, Tally, or another simple feedback form:

1. How easy was FlightSmart to use? (1–5)
2. Did you understand why the top flight was recommended? (Yes / Partly / No)
3. Was the Japanese/English wording natural and understandable? (1–5)
4. Did the traveler-priority choice reflect what matters to you? (Yes / Partly / No)
5. Was the FlightSmart Score useful? (1–5)
6. Did you understand that historical data does not predict a future delay? (Yes / No)
7. Which recommendation factor mattered most to you?
8. What was confusing?
9. What feature would make you use FlightSmart again?
10. Would you use FlightSmart before your next U.S.–Japan trip? (Definitely / Maybe / No)

## What counts as a promising beta

Do not optimize for compliments. A useful early signal is if most testers can explain *why* FlightSmart selected the top itinerary and at least several say they would use it again before a real trip.
